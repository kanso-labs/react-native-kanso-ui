# Privacy

This Storybook uses Google Analytics for anonymous usage statistics: it counts
visits, so Kanso Labs can see which pages are read. There are no accounts here
and no ads. Nothing that names you is collected; Google Analytics tells one
browser from another by a random ID, not by who is using it.

## What is recorded

The pages you open and the page that led you here; reaching the end of a page;
clicks on links that leave the site; and files you download. With each, Google
Analytics records your browser, device, language and approximate location, which
it derives from your IP address without storing the address. No user ID is set.
The address of a page records the values you give a story's controls, so those
are recorded with it; nothing else you type is sent.

Google signals and every advertising feature are off, and the site tells Google
that consent for ads is denied, for every visitor.

## Cookies

While analytics is on, Google Analytics sets two cookies on this site alone,
`_ga` and one beginning `_ga_`, to tell a returning browser from a new one. Each
Kanso Labs site keeps its own, so your choice here applies to this site only. It
is kept in your browser's local storage, and goes nowhere else.

## How long it is kept

Google Analytics deletes the records of each visit after 14 months. The totals
in its reports, such as how many times a page was read in a month, are kept.

## Your choice

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the site asks
before it loads Google Analytics, and nothing is sent until you allow it. A
static site cannot see where you are, so it goes by your browser's time zone,
and asks whenever the zone leaves it unsure. Elsewhere analytics is on until you
turn it off. If your browser keeps the time of another place while you are in
one of those, Google still keeps its cookies off for you, by your IP address,
though it may receive the visit without them; turning analytics off here stops
that too.
